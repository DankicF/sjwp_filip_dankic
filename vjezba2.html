My top 3 movies of all-time

1. The Shawshank Redemption (1994)

Directed by Frank Darabont, this film tells the story of a man named Andy Dufresne

2. The Godfather (1972)

Directed by Francis Ford Coppola, this film tells the story of the Corleone family over three generations.

3. The Dark Knight (2008)

Directed by Christopher Nolan, this film tells the story of Batman as he battles the Joker in Gotham City.
